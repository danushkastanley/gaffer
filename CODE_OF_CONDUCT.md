# Code of conduct

This project should be a place where anyone can take part without being harassed or belittled.

## What is expected

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good faith, and remember that people have different levels of experience and different first languages.
- Criticise ideas and changes, not people. Give reasons and evidence.
- Accept feedback on your own work in the same spirit.
- Keep discussion on the project.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks or jokes about anyone's personal characteristics or background.
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Persistent disruption of issues, reviews or discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere else someone is acting on behalf of the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks these expectations, report it privately: use the repository's private reporting form (the **Security** tab, then **Report a vulnerability**) and say that it is a conduct report, or contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/danushkastanley). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow this code of conduct.
